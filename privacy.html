Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Killovki is a Telegram bot that helps users create and manage short-form video content and publish it to TikTok.

Information We Collect

Killovki may process information required to provide its services, including Telegram account information and information necessary to connect a user's TikTok account.

TikTok Information

When a user connects a TikTok account, Killovki may receive information authorized by the user through TikTok's OAuth authorization process.

We only use authorized information to provide the functionality requested by the user.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, provide requested features, manage video content, and communicate with users about the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide the requested service or when required by law.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the service.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the application administrator through the Killovki Telegram bot.
